# Proposal structure

Produce Markdown in exactly this order. Every section is mandatory. Never leave
a placeholder in the output — if you lack the information, put the question in
`openQuestions` and write the section around what you do know.

## 1. What you told us
Restate their situation in their own words, drawn from the call transcript.
This is the section they check for whether we listened. No selling here.

## 2. What we recommend
The approach, in plain language. Tie each element to something they said.

## 3. Scope of work
Itemised deliverables. Each line: what it is, what they get, when.

## 4. What is not included
Explicit exclusions. This section prevents the scope argument later — never omit it.

## 5. Timeline
Phases with week numbers, plus what we need from them and by when.

## 6. Investment
Line items with prices from `pricing.md`, subtotal, total. State payment terms.

## 7. Assumptions
Every assumption the price depends on. If an assumption is wrong the price changes,
and this section is what makes that a conversation rather than a dispute.

## 8. Next step
One concrete action with a date.
