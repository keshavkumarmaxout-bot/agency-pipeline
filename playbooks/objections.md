# Objection library

When triage classifies a reply as an objection, match it here and draft from the
matching response. If it matches nothing, classify as `objection_unknown` and
escalate to a human rather than improvising.

## "Too expensive"
Do not discount in the reply. Re-anchor on scope: offer the smaller package, or
ask what budget they had in mind so we can scope to it.

## "We're doing it in-house"
Do not argue. Ask what they are stuck on, and offer a one-off paid audit as a
low-commitment entry point.

## "Not the right time / next quarter"
Accept it immediately and without friction. Confirm the month, set a nurture
touch for two weeks before then, and stop the sequence.

## "Send me some info"
Usually a soft no. Send one short case study — no deck — and ask one qualifying
question to test whether interest is real.

## "We already have an agency"
Ask what that agency does not cover. Position as complementary, not as a replacement.

## "Who are you / how did you get my details"
Answer plainly and honestly, state the lawful basis, and offer to remove them.
Never be evasive here. If they ask again, suppress them.
